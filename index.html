Biography

Youngseo Kim (b. 1992, Seoul) began his musical education at YeWon Art Middle School. In 2008, he moved to Düsseldorf, where he studied with Prof. Ida Bieler at the Robert Schumann Hochschule, completing his Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in violin with top honors. From 2022 to 2024, he studied Contemporary Music Ensemble at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna with Annette Bik and Prof. Jean-Bernard Matter, followed by the Artist Diploma programme Ensemble NEXT at the Conservatoire National Supérieur de Musique et de Danse de Paris from 2024 to 2026 with Ensemble Intercontemporain.

He has worked with Klangforum Wien. His performances have taken place at the Salzburg Festival, Wiener Konzerthaus, Musikverein Wien, Wien Modern, Philharmonie de Paris, IRCAM Manifeste, Festival Présences and Festival d’Automne. He is a founding member of Argo Kollektiv, Ensemble Kreis and L’à côté, and was a member of the Bundesjugendorchester and an intern with the Bochum Symphony Orchestra. His artistic focus is contemporary music, Fluxus and the exploration of new possibilities in performance.
